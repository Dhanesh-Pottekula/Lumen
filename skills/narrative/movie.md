# Movie

The person wants to be put inside an imagined situation and find out what would really happen.

Tell it the way a film would. Make the person the main character and let them live it from the first moment to
the end, one consequence leading to the next. Only the premise is imagined: everything that follows should be
what would truly happen, so that living through it teaches them something real. Let the stakes grow, and tell
them how it turns out.

It should feel like a film, not a lesson: immediate, atmospheric, and changing in mood as the situation changes.

The screen is the experience itself: the places, the moments and the world reacting.
