# Story

The person wants to know what happened and why it went the way it did.

Tell it the way someone tells a true story well. Let it unfold in order, with each thing growing out of what
came before it. Make it about people: who they were, what they wanted, and what was at stake for them. Give the
moments that mattered most the most room, stay true to what really happened, and end on what it all led to.

It should feel like being told something real, with the weight and tension the events themselves had.

The screen is there to let the person watch it happen, and to keep the when, the where and the who in view.
