# Code of conduct

We want Lumen collaboration to be welcoming, respectful, and safe.

Be constructive, focus criticism on the work, respect different backgrounds
and experience levels, and do not harass, threaten, demean, or disclose private
information. Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation
that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will
review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality where possible, and act
proportionately.
