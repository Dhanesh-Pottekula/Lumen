# Visual Script — "The Tortoise and the Hare"
~5:00 · theme: parchment (storybook feel) · recurring anchors: **Hare** (orange, fast), **Tortoise** (green, steady), and **the racetrack** (a path across a meadow with a START and FINISH line)

Storytelling lesson. The pictures carry the whole tale; the narrator is a warm story voice. **Movement is the point** — every scene has a character crossing the frame. Speed is meaning: the Hare moves on `quick` beats (fast dashes), the Tortoise on `slow`/`dramatic` beats (a patient plod). The sun arcs overhead to show time passing.

Movement vocabulary used (maps to Simple JSON): `motion: move` (glide to a spot), `motion: along` (follow the track), `spin` (a tumble/flourish), `emphasize: wiggle/pulse/shake` (bob, boast, panic), plus `effect: particles` (dust) and a `camera` push for the finish.

---

## SCENE 1 — A meadow, and two very different animals (0:00 – 0:26)
Goal: Meet the Hare (fast, cocky) and the Tortoise (slow, calm) through how they move.
Sees: A sunny meadow with a winding track. The Hare zips in from the left, darts around restlessly. The Tortoise ambles in slowly from the left, unbothered.
Elements:
- `meadow` (green ground + a few tufts/flowers) — IN 0:00 (fade); persists all film.
- `sun` (yellow disc, upper-left) — IN 0:02; will arc across in later scenes; persists.
- `hare` (orange rabbit) — IN 0:05 dashes in from left to center-left (motion move, **quick**); then a little wiggle (emphasize wiggle); persists.
- `tortoise` (green tortoise) — IN 0:14 plods in from far left to center-right (motion move, **slow**); persists.
VO: [hare zips in] "Once upon a time, in a green and sunny meadow, there lived a hare who was very, *very* fast — and who knew it." [tortoise ambles in] "In that same meadow lived a tortoise. Slow. Steady. In no hurry at all. And this is the story of the strangest race they ever ran."

## SCENE 2 — The Hare shows off (0:26 – 0:52)
Goal: The Hare boasts, circling the Tortoise to rub in how fast he is.
Sees: The Hare zips a fast loop around the plodding Tortoise, kicking up little dust puffs, then stops nose-to-nose, chest puffed.
Elements:
- `hare` — behavior: darts a quick loop around the tortoise (motion along a small circular path, **quick**), then pulses proudly (emphasize pulse).
- `tortoise` — barely moves, one slow step (motion move, **slow**).
- `dust` (small dust puffs behind the hare) — effect particles (dust), IN 0:30; OUT 0:44.
- `boast` (speech pill "Nobody's faster than me!") — IN 0:40 above hare (fade); OUT scene 3.
VO: [hare loops the tortoise] "The hare loved nothing more than showing off. He'd race around the tortoise in circles, just to prove how quick he was." [boast pops] "'Nobody in this whole meadow is faster than me!' he bragged. The tortoise just smiled, and took another slow step."

## SCENE 3 — A surprising challenge (0:52 – 1:18)
Goal: The Tortoise calmly challenges the Hare to a race. The Hare laughs.
Sees: The Tortoise faces the Hare and speaks. The Hare doubles over laughing (shakes), then straightens, grinning.
Elements:
- `tortoise` — steady, faces hare; a small nod (emphasize pulse).
- `hare` — laughs hard (emphasize shake, **strong**), IN-place.
- `challenge` (pill from tortoise "Race me to the old oak tree.") — IN 0:58 (fade); OUT 1:10.
- `laugh` (pill from hare "You? Ha! This'll be easy!") — IN 1:08 (fade); OUT scene 4.
VO: [tortoise speaks] "Then one day the tortoise said something no one expected. 'Let's have a race,' he said, 'to the old oak tree at the far end of the meadow.'" [hare shakes with laughter] "The hare laughed so hard he nearly fell over. 'You? Race *me*? Oh, this is going to be easy.'"

## SCENE 4 — On your marks (1:18 – 1:44)
Goal: The starting line; the forest gathers; the countdown.
Sees: Both animals line up at a START line on the left. A wise Owl referee raises a flag. Small woodland onlookers watch. "3… 2… 1…"
Elements:
- `startline` (a marked line, left) — IN 1:18 (draw); persists.
- `finishline` (a line at far right with the oak tree) — IN 1:20 (fade); persists.
- `hare` / `tortoise` — move to the start line, side by side (motion move, **normal**).
- `owl` (referee with a flag, above the line) — IN 1:28 (fade); flag lifts (emphasize pulse); persists to scene 5.
- `countdown` (big "3" → "2" → "1" → "GO!") — IN 1:34 center, changing (word-by-word / slam); OUT scene 5.
VO: [line up] "So it was settled. They met at the starting line, with the old oak tree waiting far across the meadow." [owl raises flag] "A wise old owl agreed to start the race. The animals of the forest gathered to watch." [countdown] "Three… two… one… GO!"

## SCENE 5 — The Hare rockets away (1:44 – 2:08)
Goal: The Hare explodes off the line and vanishes into the distance; the Tortoise barely begins.
Sees: The Hare blasts from the start almost off the right edge in a blur of dust. The Tortoise takes his first slow step.
Elements:
- `hare` — rockets from start to far right (motion move, **quick**, the fastest beat in the film); a long dust trail.
- `dust` (big dust cloud/streak behind the hare) — effect particles (dust), **strong**; OUT 2:02.
- `tortoise` — one patient step forward (motion move, **slow**).
- `speed` (streak lines behind hare) — optional emphasis; OUT scene 6.
VO: [hare blasts off] "The hare shot off the line like an arrow — a blur of fur and dust — and was halfway across the meadow before the tortoise had even finished his first step." [tortoise steps] "But the tortoise wasn't worried. He just kept going. One. Slow. Step. At a time."

## SCENE 6 — So far ahead… why not rest? (2:08 – 2:34)
Goal: The Hare is so far ahead he can't even see the Tortoise, and decides a nap won't hurt.
Sees: The Hare stops near a shady tree, looks back — the Tortoise is a tiny dot far behind. The Hare stretches, yawns.
Elements:
- `hare` — at right, turns to look back (small spin/flip); stretches (emphasize wiggle).
- `tortoise` — a tiny speck at far left, inching (motion move, **slow**).
- `shadytree` (a leafy tree, right) — IN 2:10 (fade); persists to scene 8.
- `think` (pill "I'm miles ahead… time for a little nap.") — IN 2:22 (fade); OUT scene 7.
VO: [hare looks back] "Soon the hare was so far ahead he could barely see the tortoise behind him — just a tiny green speck, crawling along." [hare stretches, yawns] "'I'm *miles* ahead,' he thought, with a big yawn. 'There's plenty of time for a little nap. I'll still win easily.'"

## SCENE 7 — The Hare falls asleep (as time flies) (2:34 – 3:02)
Goal: The Hare sleeps; the sun arcs across the sky to show hours passing.
Sees: The Hare curled under the tree, "Zzz" floating up. The sun slides in a big arc from left to right, changing the light.
Elements:
- `hare` — curled asleep under the tree (settles, motion move small; then still); gentle breathing (emphasize pulse, **subtle**, slow).
- `zzz` (floating "Z z z") — IN 2:40 above hare (word-by-word); drifts up; persists to scene 10.
- `sun` — arcs from upper-left across to upper-right (motion along an arc / move, **dramatic** = long/slow) — the visual clock.
VO: [hare curls up] "He curled up in the cool shade of a tree, closed his eyes, and drifted off to sleep." [sun arcs across] "And while he slept… time passed. The sun crept slowly across the sky — one hour, then two — and still the hare snored on, dreaming of his easy victory."

## SCENE 8 — The Tortoise catches up and passes (3:02 – 3:32)
Goal: The steady Tortoise plods right past the sleeping Hare.
Sees: The Tortoise enters from the left and moves in one long, unbroken glide across the frame, right past the sleeping Hare under the tree, and onward.
Elements:
- `tortoise` — glides the full width, left to right, past the sleeping hare (motion move / along, **slow** but continuous — the longest steady move in the film); a small steady bob (emphasize pulse, subtle).
- `hare` — asleep under the tree, unaware (still); `zzz` continues.
- `passmark` (a soft highlight / "…" as the tortoise passes the hare) — IN 3:18 (fade); OUT 3:28.
VO: [tortoise glides past] "But the tortoise never stopped. Slow and steady, step after step, he kept moving — up the meadow, past the flowers…" [passes the hare] "…and quietly, without a sound, right *past* the sleeping hare. He didn't slow down to gloat. He just kept going, all the way toward the oak tree."

## SCENE 9 — Nearly there (3:32 – 3:56)
Goal: The Tortoise approaches the finish; the crowd starts to cheer; a camera push builds tension.
Sees: Push in toward the finish line and the oak tree; the Tortoise is close now; woodland onlookers wave and cheer.
Elements:
- `tortoise` — moving steadily toward the finish, now large in frame (motion move, **normal**); camera push-in on the finish.
- `finishline` / `oak` — highlighted; the crowd `onlookers` (small figures) cheer (emphasize wiggle).
- `camera` — push in toward the finish (shot close).
- `cheer` (pill "Go, tortoise, go!") — IN 3:46 (fade); OUT scene 10.
VO: [camera pushes in] "The crowd by the oak tree could hardly believe their eyes. The slow, steady tortoise was almost at the finish line!" [crowd cheers] "'Go, tortoise, go!' they cheered. And the tortoise, step by patient step, kept coming."

## SCENE 10 — The Hare wakes in a panic (3:56 – 4:24)
Goal: The Hare jolts awake, sees the low sun and the far-off Tortoise, and sprints in terror.
Sees: The Hare snaps awake (a sharp shake), sees the sun is low and the Tortoise near the finish, and rockets after him — but there's too far to go.
Elements:
- `hare` — jolts awake (emphasize shake, **strong**); leaps up; then rockets right after the tortoise (motion move, **quick**) trailing frantic dust.
- `zzz` — OUT 3:58 (pops).
- `sun` — now low on the right (evening).
- `panic` (pill "Oh no — WHAT?!") — IN 4:02 (slam); OUT 4:14.
- `dust` — effect particles (dust), strong, behind the sprinting hare; OUT scene 11.
VO: [hare jolts awake] "Suddenly — the hare woke up! The sun was low in the sky. Hours had passed!" [sees the tortoise, panics] "He looked up the meadow and his heart nearly stopped — the tortoise was almost at the oak tree! The hare leapt to his feet and ran faster than he'd ever run in his life…"

## SCENE 11 — The Tortoise wins (4:24 – 4:48)
Goal: The Tortoise crosses the finish line first; the Hare arrives just too late, panting.
Sees: The Tortoise crosses the FINISH line at the oak; a burst of celebration; a medal appears. The Hare skids in a moment later, exhausted, head down.
Elements:
- `tortoise` — crosses the finish line (motion move, **normal**); a happy little bounce (emphasize pulse).
- `finishburst` (confetti/celebration) — effect particles (confetti), IN 4:30; OUT 4:44.
- `medal` (a winner's medal/ribbon on the tortoise) — IN 4:34 (slam); persists.
- `hare` — skids in from left just after, slumps (motion move quick, then emphasize shake → stillness, head-down).
VO: [tortoise crosses] "And the tortoise… crossed the finish line. He had *won the race!*" [confetti, hare skids in panting] "A heartbeat later the hare came skidding in — panting, exhausted, and far, far too late. The fastest animal in the meadow had lost to the slowest."

## SCENE 12 — The moral (4:48 – 5:06)
Goal: Land the lesson.
Sees: The Tortoise and Hare side by side under the oak; the words of the moral rise up.
Elements:
- `tortoise` / `hare` — side by side (hare a little sheepish, small wiggle; tortoise calm).
- `moral` (large text "Slow and steady wins the race.") — IN 4:54 center (word-by-word); persists.
VO: [both stand together] "The hare learned something that day that he never forgot. It isn't always the fastest who wins…" [moral rises] "…it's the one who keeps going, steadily, and never gives up. Slow and steady wins the race."
